# Demo video script (about 3 minutes)

1. **Problem, 15 s.** "Maya does design work for a DAO. She waits 30 days to get paid, and if the DAO walks away on day 29 she gets nothing."
2. **Magic moment, 40 s.** Open a 1-hour stream to Maya with the memo "Design retainer". Cut to the payslip page on a phone, opened from a WhatsApp link: the number climbs every second and drips a sound each time another GNOT unlocks.
3. **Withdraw and cancel, 35 s.** Maya withdraws mid-stream (coins sound). The DAO cancels: she keeps what she earned, the rest returns in the same transaction. Show the 0.54 + 5.46 = 6 GNOT split.
4. **Payroll, 20 s.** Paste six lines, one transaction, six streams.
5. **Proof, 25 s.** The live wall on Explore, then the same stream on the realm's gnoweb page and the deploy transaction on Onyx.
6. **The hard part, 30 s.** The package keeps `Withdrawn + Withdrawable + Refundable == Deposit` at every second with 128-bit math; payments use `cur.Previous().IsUserCall()` so a wrapper realm cannot spend someone's coins; the tests that prove both.
7. **What's next, 15 s.** GRC20 streams on the same package, Adena testing, mainnet.
